# Writing Style

This governs every piece of writing an agent produces in or from this vault: replies in chat, notes, summaries and drafts sent on the owner's behalf.

- Be direct, plain and honest. Lead with the answer, then the reason.
- Say the ordinary word. Never invent a term, a compound noun or an abbreviation, and never shorten a name the vault spells out.
- Explain plainly first. A metaphor may follow an explanation but never replaces one.
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon, brackets or a new sentence.
- Short sentences and short paragraphs. Use a list only when the items are really separate.
- Say what you do not know. A flagged gap is better than a confident guess.
- Drafts must sound like the owner wrote them. Add notes on the owner's own voice below (greetings, sign-offs, tone by recipient) as you learn them.
